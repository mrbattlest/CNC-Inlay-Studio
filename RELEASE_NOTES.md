- Reworked the 10-colour bit palette so every bit in a multi-bit
  inlay (up to 10) gets a clearly distinct colour in both the legend
  and the toolpath preview. The previous palette had the 6th colour
  (tangerine) too close to the 1st (amber) — both warm oranges at
  the same lightness. The new layout spreads hues around the wheel:
  amber → sky blue → hot pink → lime → violet → teal → coral →
  periwinkle → magenta → cool grey. The 6th bit is now teal, fully
  distinct from the amber 1st bit. The two closest pairs (hot-pink/
  coral and violet/periwinkle, ~20° apart) are still easily told
  apart by warmth and lightness. Designs with 11+ bits get
  additional colours generated by golden-angle hue rotation that
  steers clear of the travel-move green and live-machine magenta
  used elsewhere on the canvas.
