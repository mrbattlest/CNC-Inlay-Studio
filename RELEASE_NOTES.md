- Cuts now finish with a celebration dialog 🎉. Each completion
  (Pocket, Plug, or Surfacing) pops a modal showing the side that
  just finished, total run time, and a "what's next" hint:
    • Pocket → mount plug stock, re-zero, click Plug → Run.
    • Plug → glue + clamp, then plane (or use the Surface plug
      backing tool) to bring the back flush.
    • Surfacing → pop the panel out and admire your inlay.
  The dialog raises + activates the app window so the celebration
  is visible even if you've stepped away and the app is in the
  background during a long cut.
