- **Finished tab now zooms, pans, and supports area feedback.**
  - **Scroll** to zoom in/out (centred on the cursor).
  - **Drag** to pan around when zoomed in.
  - **Double-click** to reset back to fit-to-window.
  - **Shift+drag** a rectangle to capture an area-feedback snippet —
    same workflow as the Pocket and Plug tabs, but because the
    Finished view shows the assembled inlay the snippet covers **both
    sides at once**: it lists the pocket toolpaths AND the plug
    toolpaths touching that zone (the plug paths are looked up in
    their mirrored frame automatically), so a single selection gives
    a complete picture of how the two halves meet there.

  A hint line at the top of the Finished tab spells out the controls.
